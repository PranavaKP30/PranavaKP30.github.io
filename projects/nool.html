Personal project

Nool

Personal project · Machine learning, forecasting, full-stack app

The Problem

Nool came from my grandfather's shop in Tamil Nadu. Like a lot of small shop owners, he kept track of stock mostly in his head: what's selling, what's running low, and which suppliers actually show up on time. When something ran out, there wasn't a good way to know if ordering it today meant it'd be back in two days or two weeks.

The Process

  • Demand forecasting: an ML model learns each product's sales pattern and predicts what the shop will need.
  • Inventory: the app tracks current stock and flags what should be reordered before it runs out.
  • Supplier tracking: Nool keeps a record of how each supplier has actually performed, so the owner knows who's reliable and how long a restock really takes.
  • Restock estimates: putting demand and supplier history together, it tells the owner when an item will be back in stock if they order now.

The Outcome

Nool taught me to think about who's actually using what I build. A forecast doesn't help if a busy shop owner can't read it in a few seconds, and I try to keep that in mind with hardware too.
