Personal project

CareWatch

Team lead and developer · 2026 · ESP32 firmware, sensors, cloud, mobile app

The Problem

People living with dementia can wander or have health emergencies without anyone knowing. Caregivers need a simple way to know where their loved ones are and whether they're okay, without a complicated device.

The Process

  • Wearable firmware: an ESP32-S3 cellular board with non-blocking sensor polling, self-registration pairing, and a simple button-based interface.
  • Location and safety: GPS tracking with geofenced safety zones and an SOS button.
  • Health sensing: a pulse-oximetry sensor for heart rate and blood-oxygen monitoring. EDIT: confirm what's finished vs. in progress.
  • Cloud and app: a cloud backend that tracks device status and sends alerts to a Flutter caregiver app.

The Outcome

CareWatch started as a team project I led at NYU Tandon and grew into my first full system spanning hardware, firmware, cloud, and app. It's where I first learned that a wearable is only as good as its reliability and battery life.
