About me

Hi, I'm Pranav.

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Georgia Tech, concentrating in Circuit Technology and Bioengineering. I'm from Franklin, Massachusetts, and expect to graduate in December 2028.

What drew me to EE is that you can work at every level, from code all the way down to transistors. Firmware runs on a microcontroller, that microcontroller sits on a PCB, and the chip itself is analog and digital circuits built out of transistors. I've mostly worked on boards and firmware so far, but I want to learn the rest too, like Verilog, FPGAs, and analog and digital IC design.

Most of my experience so far has been in research. I'm the hardware engineer on a wearable system at Georgia Tech's Neuroloops Lab, and I've built real-time software and device drivers for human experiments at Northeastern's Movement Neuroscience Lab. Because these systems are used on real people, I've learned to think about safety and reliability while I'm designing, instead of after.

The work I enjoy most is when analog circuits, digital logic, and firmware all have to work together on one system. That's usually where things break, and figuring out why is what keeps me interested. I want to keep doing that kind of work, whether it's in medical devices, wearables, or any other hardware product.

Skills

  • Hardware design: schematics, PCB and flex PCB layout, analog front ends, power electronics
  • Embedded and software: C++, Python, ESP32, real-time Linux, SPI/I2C/UART
  • Lab and test: oscilloscopes, multimeters, board bring-up and debugging, hardware validation
  • Systems: biosignal acquisition, closed-loop control, real-time systems
